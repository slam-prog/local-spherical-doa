# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-17

### Added

- Eight-microphone spherical-array simulation.
- Local 45-degree field-of-view estimator.
- GCC-PHAT delay estimation.
- Sub-sample peak interpolation.
- Coarse-to-fine angular search.
- Continuous refinement.
- Monte Carlo benchmark.
- Angular error statistics.
- Worst-case trial logging.
- Results for 10, 20, and 30 dB SNR.

### Known limitations

- Idealized far-field model.
- No reverberation.
- No hardware measurement.
- No microphone mismatch model.
- No anatomical localization.
